Studio Uploader

Studio Uploader is a small private tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channels, using the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service: it runs only on the owner's computer, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

It uploads videos the owner has made and approved, sets their title, description, thumbnail and schedule, and marks AI-assisted content with YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting.
