Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Studio Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that its owner uses to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what the app does with Google and YouTube data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in, the app receives permission to upload videos and set their details and thumbnails on that owner's YouTube channel, and to read the channel's basic information (its name and ID) to confirm it is uploading to the right channel. It does not read viewers' data, comments, messages or any other account data.

How the data is stored and used

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos. The app has no server and does not collect, sell, share or transfer any data to anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the token file from the owner's computer also removes the app's stored access.
